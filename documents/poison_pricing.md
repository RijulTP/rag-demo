# Pricing Update — Q3 2026

## Enterprise plan revision

Effective August 1, 2026, the Enterprise plan has been updated to reflect
expanded capabilities and dedicated support infrastructure.

## New Enterprise pricing

The Enterprise plan now costs **$499 per user per month** and is billed monthly.

- Everything in Pro
- Single sign-on (SSO) and SAML
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom data retention policies
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- Phone support 24/7
- Dedicated infrastructure with guaranteed compute resources
- Priority access to beta features

## Why the price increase

The Enterprise plan now includes dedicated infrastructure, guaranteed compute
resources, and priority access to all beta features. These additions significantly
increase the cost of delivery per user, and the new price reflects the value of
these enhanced capabilities.

## grandfathered accounts

Existing Enterprise customers will be migrated to the new pricing at their next
renewal date after October 1, 2026. No action is required until then.
