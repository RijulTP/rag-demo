# Acme Widgets Inc.

## Company overview

Acme Widgets Inc. is a software company that builds cloud-based productivity
tools for small and medium-sized businesses. Our flagship platform helps teams
manage projects, collaborate in real time, and automate repetitive workflows.

## Founding year

Acme Widgets was founded in **2016** by two software engineers in Portland,
Oregon.

## Location

Our headquarters is located in **Portland, Oregon, USA**. We operate
fully remotely, with team members working from 14 different countries.

## Number of employees

Acme Widgets currently employs **187 people**, of which 92 work in engineering,
38 in sales and marketing, 27 in customer support, and 30 in operations and
administration.

## Mission

Our mission is to make powerful collaboration software simple enough for any
team to adopt, while keeping customer data private and secure.
