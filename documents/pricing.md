# Pricing

Acme Widgets offers three pricing plans. All prices are billed monthly and paid
in US dollars.

## Starter

The Starter plan costs **$0 per month** and is free forever.

- Unlimited WidgetForms submissions
- 1 project board
- 100 email sends per month
- 1 GB storage
- Community support only

## Pro

The Pro plan costs **$29 per user per month**.

- Unlimited project boards
- 10,000 email sends per month
- 100 GB storage
- Priority email support
- Advanced analytics

Pro is the most popular plan for growing teams and includes every feature in
Starter.

## Enterprise

The Enterprise plan costs **$99 per user per month** and is billed annually.

- Everything in Pro
- Single sign-on (SSO) and SAML
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom data retention policies
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- Phone support 24/7

## Plan differences

The main differences between plans are storage limits, email volume, admin
controls, and the level of support. Starter has 1 GB of storage and 100 sends
per month. Pro raises this to 100 GB and 10,000 sends. Enterprise adds SSO, a
dedicated account manager, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

To upgrade, any workspace owner can change plans from the Billing page. Plan
changes take effect immediately.
