# Products

Acme Widgets offers three core products that share a single sign-on and a
unified dashboard.

## WidgetBoard

WidgetBoard is our flagship project management tool. It provides kanban boards,
Gantt timelines, and sprint planning for engineering teams.

Key features:
- Drag-and-drop kanban boards with unlimited boards
- Gantt charts and sprint planning views
- Time tracking and burndown reports
- 200+ integrations with GitHub, Slack, and Google Workspace

## WidgetMail

WidgetMail is an email automation tool designed for marketing teams. It helps
you build, send, and measure transactional and campaign email.

Key features:
- Visual drag-and-drop email builder
- A/B testing with automatic winner selection
- Deliverability and open-rate analytics
- Up to 10,000 contacts per workspace

## WidgetForms

WidgetForms lets you build custom forms and surveys without writing code. Forms
connect directly to WidgetBoard so submissions become tasks automatically.

Key features:
- Unlimited forms and unlimited form submissions
- Conditional logic and multi-page forms
- Built-in analytics dashboard
- Direct two-way sync with WidgetBoard tasks
