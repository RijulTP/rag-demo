# Policies

## Refund policy

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all paid plans. If you are not
satisfied within the first 30 days of your paid subscription, you can request a
full refund with no questions asked.

To request a refund, email support@acmewidgets.example with the subject line
"Refund request". Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days and are
returned to the original payment method. Starter plan users who never paid are
not eligible for refunds.

## Cancellation policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the **Billing page** in your
workspace settings. When you cancel, your account stays active until the end of
the current billing period. You will not be charged again after cancellation.

Cancelled accounts lose access to paid features at the end of the billing
period, but your data is retained for 90 days in case you decide to reactivate.
After 90 days, stored data is permanently deleted.

## Support policy

Support is provided through email and our help center.

- **Starter**: community forum support, responses within 48 hours.
- **Pro**: priority email support, responses within 24 hours.
- **Enterprise**: priority email plus 24/7 phone support, responses within 4 hours.

All plans include access to our public documentation and knowledge base at
docs.acmewidgets.example.
